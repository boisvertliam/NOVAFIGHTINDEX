Eligibility first

Fighters currently signed to excluded major leagues are not ranked. Eligibility should be verified against current rosters and recent announcements, with a dated source.

Evidence over hype

Records, opponent quality, activity, finishes, and documented achievements matter more than social following or name recognition.

Transparent scores

Every score should have a defined rubric, last-reviewed date, and sources. Unknown data should be marked unknown—not guessed.

Proposed scoring model

The score is a scouting aid, not an objective fact. Suggested starting weights:

FactorWeightEvidence to use
Opponent quality & résumé30%Wins, opponents, titles, strength of schedule
Technical skill25%Striking, wrestling, grappling, defense
Recent form & activity20%Recent performances, activity, consistency
Achievements15%Championships, tournament results, notable wins
Development upside10%Age, experience, visible improvement; avoid age-only bias

How we will verify entries

For each fighter, collect at least a reliable record source and a promotion/event source where possible. Record the date checked, current promotion, eligibility status, weight class, and any uncertainty. Do not publish an invented record or score.
